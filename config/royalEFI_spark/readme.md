## Features

- 2 Injectors
- 2 Coils
- 4 Low Side Switch
- 1 VR / 2 Hall Triggers
- 2 Digital Inputs
- 4 Analogue 0-5v inputs
- Wideband (14point7)
- 1 Canbus
- 4bar map
- Onboard Barometer
- Onboard Removable SD Card
- Bluetooth
